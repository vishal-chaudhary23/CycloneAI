## Demo Overview

The demo video presents the working prototype of **Cyclone Intelligence AI**, developed by **Team AIMERS**.

The video demonstrates the complete workflow from cyclone data input to AI-based forecasting and India-focused risk visualization.

## What the Video Shows

### 1. Team & Project Introduction

A brief introduction to **Team AIMERS** and the **Cyclone Intelligence AI** project.

The project focuses on AI-based tropical cyclone forecasting, including:

- Track prediction
- Intensity prediction
- Rapid Intensification detection
- Predictive uncertainty
- India-focused risk assessment

### 2. Input Data

The video demonstrates the satellite observations and historical cyclone motion data used by the system.

The model works with temporal cyclone observations to capture the evolution of the cyclone over time.
